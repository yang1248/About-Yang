A food-delivery management system for campus dining, bringing menu maintenance, order processing, and business reporting into one administration interface.

## Background

Campus dining operations involve staff, dishes, set meals, orders, and store availability. Administrators need to maintain the menu, follow orders through their lifecycle, and keep track of business activity.

This project brings core data management, order operations, and reporting together in a single admin system, keeping related information and everyday workflows organized.

## Project overview

- **Development date**: October 2025.
- **My role**: Project lead.
- **Technology stack**: Spring Boot, Redis, SSM, Vue.js, Element UI, and MySQL.

## Core features

### 01 · Core data management

Manage employees, categories, dishes, and set meals. Employee management supports search, creation, and disabling accounts. Category management organizes dishes and set meals, while menu management supports creating, viewing, updating, deleting, and changing sale availability.

### 02 · Orders and store operations

Process customer orders with search, cancellation, dispatch, and completion actions. Voice notifications draw attention to incoming orders, and store settings switch between open and closed states.

  <img src="/works/campus-delivery/screenshot-02.png" alt="Store status dialog with open and closed options and a confirmation button" width="458" height="507" loading="lazy" />

### 03 · Business reporting

Summarize business activity with charts for metrics such as revenue and user counts. Excel report exports support further review and analysis.

The dashboard shown at the top of this page brings together daily metrics, order statuses, and dish and set-meal summaries.
